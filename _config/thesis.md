## Target Setup Types

### 1. Distribution Shorts (Primary)

Look for tokens where price is rallying on negative OBV — smart money selling into retail buying. The best distribution shorts have:

- OBV divergence on 2+ timeframes (price up, OBV down)
- Exchange inflows above 2x average on the rally
- Smart money/Top PnL traders showing net outflows
- 4H RSI overbought (>70) in a weekly bearish regime
- Crowded perps longs (>5:1 ratio) creating cascade risk

**Current distribution targets:** ETH
**Scan criteria for /hunt-squeezes:** Crowded longs with 5:1+ imbalance on perps

### 2. Accumulation Longs (Selective)

Exception trades in risk-off. Only valid when institutional on-chain evidence overwhelms the bearish macro. Required:

- Named fund positions (not anonymous wallets) building from zero in <30 days
- Multiple funds (3+) independently accumulating — not one fund, multiple
- Total fund position value >$20M (scale matters — $2M positions don't move markets)
- Exchange outflows confirming tokens leaving CEXs
- On-chain signal must override bearish TA per signal hierarchy

**Current accumulation targets:** HYPE
**Watchlist candidates:** ZRO (Wintermute loading, Alpha Score 8/12), AERO (needs 2nd fund convergence)

### 3. Breakout Plays (Opportunistic)

Momentum continuation trades where price breaks a structural level on expanding volume. Valid in any regime — breakouts work in bear markets (breakdown shorts) and bull markets (breakout longs).

**Qualifying breakout long:**
- Price closes above a 3+ touch resistance level
- Volume on the break is 1.5x+ period average (OBV confirming)
- ADX rising above 25 (trend is real, not a fakeout)
- BB squeeze preceded the breakout (coiled energy releasing)
- On-chain neutral or better — exchange outflows, no smart money selling into the break

**Qualifying breakdown short (preferred in risk-off):**
- Price closes below a 3+ touch support level
- Volume on the break is 1.5x+ average
- ADX rising, DI- dominant
- On-chain shows distribution — exchange inflows, smart money exiting

**Disqualifiers:**
- Low-volume breakout (below average) — likely a fakeout. The wyckoff-analyst catches these as Upthrust/Spring events.
- Breakout against the weekly regime without on-chain support — in risk-off, a long breakout against the weekly downtrend needs institutional backing to be trusted.

**Current breakout candidates:** ZRO (broke $2.00 on Mar 16 — needs volume confirmation and pullback test)

### 4. Mean Reversion (Opportunistic)

Fade extreme overextensions where price has deviated too far from fair value. These are short-term trades (4H to daily timeframe) that work in ranging or trending markets.

**Qualifying mean reversion long (oversold bounce):**
- Daily RSI < 25 OR 4H RSI < 20
- Price at or below daily lower Bollinger Band
- OBV NOT confirming the down move (divergence = selling exhaustion)
- Near a 3+ touch support level (structural floor)
- Perps not crowded short (if shorts are already crowded, the bounce may have already happened)

**Qualifying mean reversion short (overbought fade) — preferred in risk-off:**
- Daily RSI > 75 OR 4H RSI > 80
- Price at or above daily upper Bollinger Band
- OBV divergence (price up, OBV down = distribution)
- Near a 3+ touch resistance level (structural ceiling)
- In a weekly bearish regime (mean reversion shorts WITH the trend are highest conviction)

**Key rule:** Mean reversion trades target the BB middle band (SMA 20), NOT a full trend reversal. These are 1-3R tactical trades with tight stops beyond the extreme, not position trades.

**Disqualifiers:**
- ADX > 40 in the direction of the move — a very strong trend can stay overbought/oversold far longer than expected. Don't fade a freight train.
- On-chain contradicts the fade — if smart money is aggressively buying into an oversold condition, the bounce may be the start of a real reversal, not a mean reversion.

**Current mean reversion candidates:** None active

1. **Check active theses first** — ETH, TAO, HYPE, ZRO, AERO. Have entry zones been hit? Have invalidation levels been breached?

4. **Scan for breakdowns** — any token in focus sectors that broke below 3+ touch support on 1.5x+ volume in the last 24-72h
5. **Scan for mean reversion fades** — any token with daily RSI >75 or <25 that also has OBV divergence. Overbought fades in a weekly bearish regime are highest priority.
6. **Squeeze scan for crowded positioning** — both crowded longs (short squeeze DOWN) and crowded shorts (squeeze UP). In risk-off, crowded longs on rallying tokens are the primary hunt target.
