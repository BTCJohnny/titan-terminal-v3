# Accumulation Patterns

## Purpose
Reference library of recognized pre-breakout accumulation patterns. Use these to identify which pattern a token is exhibiting and calibrate expectations for timing and magnitude.

---

## Pattern 1: Classic Accumulation

The baseline pattern — price consolidates in a tight range while volume declines and entities quietly load.

**Setup Conditions:**
- Price in tight range (±5-10% band) for 2-4 weeks
- Volume declining 20%+ below average
- Exchange outflows increasing
- Smart money/entities adding positions

**Confirmation (breakout):**
- Price breaks above consolidation range
- Volume spike 2x+ average on the breakout candle
- Entity positions at or near peak

**Target:** 2-3x the consolidation range width. A ±8% consolidation typically precedes a 16-24% move.

**Example:** ZRO — ±8% range for 49 days, breakout +62% to peak.

---

## Pattern 2: Smart Money Divergence

Price action and entity positioning diverge — price is flat or down while entities increase positions. The gap eventually closes with price catching up to positioning.

**Setup Conditions:**
- Price flat or declining
- Entity balances increasing week-over-week
- On-chain metrics improving while price lags
- Volume declining (market not paying attention)

**Trigger:**
- Gap between entity accumulation and price action closes
- Often preceded by a catalyst (news, partnership, protocol upgrade)
- Or simply runs out of sellers at depressed prices

**Example:** SOL — Wintermute went from $20M to $63M while price consolidated at $117-$128. The divergence closed with a +26% rally.

**Key insight:** The divergence tells you entities have information (or conviction) that the market hasn't priced in yet. The longer the divergence persists, the more explosive the resolution.

---

## Pattern 3: Volume Dry-Up

The simplest pattern — after a decline, volume evaporates and price stabilizes, indicating selling exhaustion.

**Setup Conditions:**
- Prior decline of 20%+ (fear/selling has occurred)
- Volume drops to 30-40%+ below average
- Price stabilizes in narrow range (sellers exhausted)
- No more selling pressure = floor established

**Confirmation:**
- Volume spike on any up-move
- First real buying volume after the dry-up = breakout signal
- Price holds above the dry-up range on pullback

**Key:** This pattern is necessary but not sufficient alone. Volume dry-up + entity accumulation = high conviction. Volume dry-up alone could just mean nobody cares (dead token).

## Pattern Comparison

| Attribute | Classic | SM Divergence | Volume Dry-Up |
|-----------|---------|---------------|---------------|
| **Timeframe** | 2-4 weeks | 2-6 weeks | 1-3 weeks |
| **Key Signal** | Range + outflows | Entity vs price gap | Volume collapse |
| **Complexity** | Low | Medium | Low |
| **False Positive Rate** | Medium | Low | High |
| **Expected Move** | 15-30% | 20-50% | 10-25% |
| **Best Tool** | Exchange flows | Entity tracking | Volume analysis |

---

## Pattern Combinations (Highest Conviction)

The strongest setups combine multiple patterns:

| Combination | Conviction | Example |
|-------------|-----------|---------|
| Classic + SM Divergence + Insider Buy | Maximum | ZRO (CEO buy + outflows + range) |
| Volume Dry-Up + SM Divergence + Entity Convergence | Very High | Multiple independent signals confirming |
| Volume Dry-Up + SM Divergence | High | SOL (volume collapse + Wintermute 3x) |
| Classic + Volume Dry-Up only | Moderate | Common but needs entity confirmation |

## Anti-Patterns (False Positives)

### Dead Token
- Volume dry-up but no entity interest
- Price stable because nobody trades it, not because supply is absorbed
- **Test:** Are any tracked entities holding? If zero entity positions, it's dead, not accumulating.

### Retail Accumulation Only
- Exchange outflows from retail wallets (not institutions)
- Top holders are exchanges (Robinhood, Coinbase) growing = retail buying
- No fund/entity positions
- **This is a distribution target**, not an accumulation setup (see LINK)

### Post-Breakout Distribution
- Entities loaded positions weeks ago
- Price has already moved 15%+
- Entities now distributing into strength
- **You're the exit liquidity.** Check entity timing carefully.

### Token Unlock Confusion
- Exchange outflows look bullish but are actually unlocked tokens being claimed
- Fresh wallet inflows at 10x+ average from vesting contracts
- **Test:** Check if the outflow source is a vesting/distribution contract.
